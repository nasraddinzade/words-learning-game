## Manual review

Fixed: six `avoid` lists listed antonyms or neighbours that do not satisfy the definition (here → there, there → here, year → month/decade, old → young, before → after, right → left). 6 of 25, all in `avoid`; definitions, sentences and translations were fine. The `avoid` lists of all 250 entries were re-read: 40 lists were trimmed in total, almost all antonym pairs (up/down, on/off, big/small, long/short, high/low, late/early, more/less, this/that, these/those, ever/never) and time units (hour → minute, month → year/week). Sixty-six entries keep an empty list, mostly grammar words that have no synonym.

Known softness:
- *i* is stored in lower case because the validator requires lower-case words. The sentence writes the gap as `{I}`, and the screens pass every word through `displayWord` (`src/app/format.ts`), which capitalises this one word; the typed answer is compared case-insensitively.
- *a* and *an* are each other's only `avoid` word; *the* has none, so *a* and *an* will appear as distractors for *the*, which is correct.
- Antonyms are deliberately not in `avoid`: *up* and *down*, *here* and *there* are good distractors because only one of them fits the sentence.
- *no* is defined as "the opposite of yes" because *not*, *none* and *nothing* all start with "no".
