## Manual review

25 random entries (seed 20261008): freeze, entertainment, fantasy, satisfy, legend, pump, debut, founder, reasonable, terrorist, rip, princess, transition, rural, designer, sheet, knee, maintenance, personally, deputy, premier, gather, cheese, revolution, convert.

7 of 25 needed a fix, all in `avoid`: *freeze* (stop), *princess* (prince, queen, duchess), *knee* (leg, elbow), *cheese* (cream), *sheet* (layer), *deputy* (vice), *founder* (father). Meanings, sentences and translations were correct. Because more than 3 of 25 were bad, the `avoid` lists of the whole batch were re-read against their definitions: 30 of 252 entries listed antonyms, parts or neighbours by topic that do not fit the definition (golf → tennis, cousin → nephew, latter → former, minority → majority, hip → waist, and so on) and were trimmed. Thirty-nine entries now keep an empty list.

Lesson carried into the next B1 batches: an `avoid` word must itself satisfy the definition; words that are merely related (opposites, siblings, parts of the same body or team) are not distractor collisions and must not be listed.

Known softness: *chest* and *bath* are defined in one sense only; *baseball* is explained very simply to pass the length rule; *hopefully* has no neighbour to list.
